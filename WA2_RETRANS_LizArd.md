# Retranslating Liz & Ard (トカ & ゲー / Toka & Gě)

Working design doc for the retranslation of Wild Arms 2's comic-relief Odessa duo.
Sources: our own EN dump (`Liz_and_Ard_EN_full.txt`), JP decode (`Toka_and_Ge_JP_full.txt`),
and localization-reception research (see WA2_NOTES / this session).

---

## The diagnosis (why the EN scenes land badly)

**It is bad *localization*, not bad *source writing*.** Three independent lines of evidence agree:

1. **Primary-source comparison (ours).** JP Toka is a *coherent, rhythmic* comic blowhard —
   e.g. 「あったまい〜ッ！そして我兄、カッチョええ〜ッ！」("So smart! And we're sooo cool!"),
   「言うこと聞くまでブチのめすッ！」("We'll beat you till you obey!"),
   「我片らはブラリきままに飛○したリザード星人なんだトカ」("we're free-wheeling Lizardians who
   just drifted here"). Punchy, tracks logically, ends on his tic. The EN turns this into
   disconnected word-salad ("Punch Drunk Momentalism," "Dizzy Lizzy!", "A homing instinct
   brought me back from a secret flower garden").
2. **Wild Arms Fandom wiki (Liz page):** "the English translation of his dialog makes him often
   prattle on meaninglessly, **which isn't quite what the writers were going for**."
3. **WA2's general localization reputation:** "literal but not streamlined," "gibberish
   pseudo-scientific techno-babble more distracting than enlightening" (Lord Spencer),
   "slides into actual incomprehensibility in denser passages" (barleybap).

**So the failure mode is:** the localizers kept the *density and flowery vocabulary* of Toka's
speech but dropped the *connective logic and rhythm* that made each riff track. Result = noise
where the JP had a character. The fix is **restore coherence + rhythm**, NOT "tone down the wacky."

## What must be PRESERVED (the EN got these right — don't undo them)

- **The name-pun.** JP トカ+ゲ = トカゲ (tokage, "lizard"); EN Liz+Ard = "Lizard." This is a genuine
  transcreation win (Ace-Attorney-tier). KEEP "Liz & Ard" — re-literalizing to "Toka & Gě" would
  *destroy* the pun for English players.
- **The double-act structure.** Toka/Liz = the talker; Gě/Ard = the straight-"man" who only ever
  says his own name, "translated" by his partner. This is intact and funny in both. Keep it.
- **Pompous-lizard register.** The core joke is a *lizard mook talking like a self-important
  aristocrat/scientist*. Keep the highfalutin diction — just make it *parse*.

## What must be FIXED

| Problem | JP reality | Retranslation rule |
|---|---|---|
| Non-sequiturs read as gibberish | JP riffs track logically & react to the scene | Every line must have a *legible referent* — he's always bragging, threatening, or reacting to something concrete. Keep the flourish, restore the thread. |
| Lost verbal tic (〜だトカ) | Every Toka line ends 〜だトカ (reinforces lizard pun + tags the comic voice) | Give Liz an **English idiolect** that stands in for the tic — see below. |
| No register contrast | Grandiose 我兄 ("we, the magnificent") vs. his lowly status | Lean the diction consistently pompous/pseudo-scholarly so the gap with his actual competence is the joke. |
| Flattened rhythm | Short punchy boasts + comic beats | Shorter sentences. Let Ard's name-only replies do comic timing. |

## Handling Toka's 〜だトカ tic in English

Japanese sentence-final tics have no direct English form (cf. Frog's mock-Elizabethan in
Chrono Trigger, Legends of Localization). Options, best first:

1. **A recurring lizard-flavored verbal signature** — end his boasts with a signature flourish
   that carries the pun forward, e.g. a hissed *"...naturally"* / *"...s'obvious"* delivered with a
   reptilian lilt, or a catchphrase sign-off (cf. Splatoon "Stay fresh!"). Low-risk, repeatable.
2. **Consistent pseudo-scientific idiolect** — he narrates everything as "science," so brand his
   register (mock-lecture cadence, "Observe!", "Scientifically speaking...") as his identifier.
   The EN already gestures at this ("children of science," "Hard SF") — make it *consistent and
   coherent* instead of random.
3. **Don't force a 1:1 tic** where it'd add noise — compensate with register instead. (The tic's
   job was partly speaker-tagging; English can tag via idiolect.)

Recommendation: **#1 + #2 combined** — a pseudo-scholarly register with a repeated lizard-lilt
sign-off. That rebuilds both the pun-reinforcement and the comic-voice-tag the だトカ did.

## Sample rewrites (illustrative — JP-grounded)

> **Intro** — JP: 「魔法のテロリスト『オデッサ』の○し○、トカとゲーとは我兄らのことよ」
> - EN (orig): "The hidden powers spreading the terrorists' spell |Odessa| through the land are Liz & Ard. That's us."
> - **Retrans:** "Behold — the shadow hand behind Odessa's grip on this land: **Liz... and Ard.** ...Yes. Us. Naturally."

> **Boast** — JP: 「我兄の来○とッ！ゲーのパワーッ！さらに○えて、我兄のカッコ○さッ！」
> - EN (orig): "I'm the brains! Ard's the power! And, I'm cool!"  *(actually close — keep the shape)*
> - **Retrans:** "The brains — *mine.* The muscle — Ard's. And the *dashing good looks*? Also mine. Obviously."

> **Gundam parody** — JP: 「ファルガイアの重力に魂引かれた」
> - **Retrans (keep the parody):** "My very soul... drawn down by Filgaia's gravity...!" *(intentional Gundam riff — flag for the reader, don't neutralize it)*

> **Nonsense to fix** — EN (orig): "Punch Drunk Momentalism." / "Dizzy Lizzy!"
> - These are battle-cry flourishes. **Retrans:** give him a *named signature move* that sounds
>   pompous-scientific but parses — e.g. "The Reptilian Gambit!" / "Behold: Tail Theory in action!"

## Open decisions (need your call)
- **How domesticated?** Ace-Attorney-heavy (fully rebuilt English gags) vs. lighter touch (fix
  coherence, keep JP flavor). I lean *moderate* — restore sense + rhythm, keep the lizard pun and
  sci-fi-alien reveal, don't invent whole new jokes.
- **Give Liz a fixed sign-off catchphrase?** (My rec: yes — one repeated lizard-lilt tag.)
- **Localize Gě/Ard's name-only gag further?** (My rec: no — "Ard...Ard!" is already perfect.)
