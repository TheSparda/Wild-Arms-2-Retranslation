# Wild Arms 2 — Japanese Script Summary (from decoded STGEVT.BIN)

**Source & confidence.** Built from our own decode of the JP disc's `STGEVT.BIN`
(`jp_script/disc1_STGEVT_decoded.txt`, 8,633 messages), currently **~92% legible** —
all kana/katakana + ~200 kanji solved. Cross-checked against swimmylionni's line-by-line
JP/EN prologue commentary and the toybox/tekeremu verbatim quote pages.

⚠️ **Accuracy caveats:**
- The script file is ordered by map/event, **not story order**, so this is a thematic/structural
  synthesis, not a linear retelling.
- The two central concept-words **英雄 (hero, codes 8a7b/8a7c)** and **生け贄 (sacrifice, 8b21)**
  were targeted-solved and now decode directly. Verified thematic line:
  「『英雄』って『生け贄』を それらしく○っただけの呼び方だとしたら、わたしは、うれしくないよ」
  = *"If 'hero' is just a fancier name dressed over 'sacrifice,' then I don't like it."*
  A minority of rarer kanji remain as `<hex>` placeholders but do not obscure the core scenes.

---

## Premise & world

The world is **ファルガイア (Filgaia)**, a dying frontier world. The party are **渡り鳥
(watari-dori, "Drifters")** — wandering adventurers-for-hire (NOT a formal military unit; the
original localization's "team/squad" framing overstates their organization). They're recruited
into **ARMS**, a small task force formed to counter the terrorist organization **オデッサ
(Odessa)**, led by **ヴィンスフェルト・ラダマンティス (Vinsfeld Rhadamanthus)**.

The world runs on **マナ (Mana)** / lay-line energy, channeled through **ガーディアン
(Guardians)** summoned via **ミーディアム (Mediums)**.

## The central theme — deconstructing "the Hero"

The spine of WA2 is an argument *about the very idea of a 英雄 (eiyū, "hero")*. This is stated
directly and repeatedly in the decoded text:

- A hero is **not something given to you, not something to rely on, not a thing with form**
  (Brad: 「英雄なんていうのは 与えてくれるモノでも、頼るモノでもない ましてや、形のあるモノでもない」).
- The "hero" is framed as a **生け贄 (ikenie, "sacrifice")** — society's instinct to pin the
  world's survival on one chosen victim rather than share the burden. Decoded line: *"if it's
  something the power of people can't handle, do we offer up a 'hero' as a sacrifice?"*
- The counter-thesis, stated by nearly every party member: **no one has the power to do
  everything alone, but everyone has the power to help someone else** (「誰も、ひとりで全てを
  こなすほどの力など持っているはずがありません…でも、誰もが、誰かを助ける力を持っている」).
  If everyone unites their hearts, *"we can protect the world without clinging to the sacrifice
  called a 'hero' — we can even make miracles happen."*

So the heroism the game endorses is **collective, chosen, and mundane** — not a destined savior.

## Party members (voice/arc, confirmed from decoded lines)

- **アシュレー・ウィンチェスター (Ashley Winchester)** — protagonist, young ARMS recruit. Becomes
  host of the demon **ナイトブレイザー (Knight Blazer)** — decoded as *"do you already have it,
  or will you gain it — awareness/resolve as the vessel of Knight Blazer."* His arc: fear of
  losing himself to that power vs. the wish to simply **return home to an ordinary life with
  Marina** (「僕が帰ってくる世界はそれだけでいい…両手いっぱいの小さな世界」). Earnest, dutiful,
  not quippy.
- **マリナ (Marina)** — Ashley's anchor at home; her endgame monologue (*"no matter how far
  apart, I'll never lose sight of you… so don't lose sight of your own place to return to"*) is
  the emotional climax of Ashley's thread.
- **リルカ・エレニアック (Lilka)** — novice sorceress, earnest and warm; wants to become like her
  big sister and *"find the spell that makes people smile."* Bright, insecure about competence,
  never ditzy.
- **ブラッド・エヴァンス (Brad Evans)** — ex-soldier "hero of Slayheim," now fugitive. Terse,
  principled, weighty. Nemesis Vinsfeld. Delivers the game's thesis speeches on what a hero is.
- **ティム・ライムレス (Tim Rhymeless)** — young Guardian medium. Refuses to be the sacrificial
  "pillar": *"even if I died and saved Filgaia, next time the pillar would be Colette — I won't
  accept that. I fight so no future is built on someone's sacrifice."* Gentle, resolute beyond
  his years.
- **カノン (Kanon)** — cyborg bounty hunter; true self **アイシャ・ベルデナット (Aisha
  Belldenat)**. Her reconciliation scene: she realizes what she wanted was never to be a "hero"
  but to have **仲間 (nakama, comrades)** — the bond, not the status. Tough register (あたし).
- **マリアベル・アーミティッジ (Mariabel/Marivel)** — ancient vampire noble, joins late. Archaic
  haughty speech (わらわ / 〜じゃ). *"A parting will come someday, but I won't regret it —
  because I wished for it."*

## Recurring foils
- **トカ & ゲー (Toka & Gě)** — comic lizard-duo mini-bosses; absurd, pun-heavy (incl. a Gundam
  parody line 「ファルガイアの重力に魂引かれた」).
- **トニー & スコット (Tony & Scott)** — deadpan comic pair with a running baseball-strike-zone bit.

## Endgame beats (from decoded late-file blocks)
- Confrontation with Vinsfeld/Odessa and the machinery of the "hero-sacrifice" system.
- Marivel's party: the goal is *not to destroy* the final Guardian/pillar but to "seal/entrust"
  it — force alone isn't the answer.
- Marina's "I'll keep protecting the place you return to" resolves Ashley's fear of Knight Blazer.
- Bittersweet victory: *"we won, so we weren't wrong — right?"* — the ending explicitly refuses
  the clean "heroes triumph" note, questioning the cost.

## What a retranslation should preserve (grounded in this decode)
1. **英雄 = "hero" as a loaded, critiqued concept** — keep the philosophical throughline; the
   original localization flattened it into generic hero-talk.
2. **渡り鳥 = "Drifters"** (good existing choice) — avoid "team/squad/unit."
3. **生け贄 = "sacrifice/scapegoat"** — the hero-as-sacrifice metaphor must land clearly.
4. **仲間 = "comrades/bonds"** as the emotional counterweight to "hero."
5. Character registers as above (Marivel archaic, Kanon tough, Ashley earnest, Brad terse).
