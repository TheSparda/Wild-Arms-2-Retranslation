## GLOSSARY / KEY TERMS (38, from guide)
- **Argetlahm** (アガートラーム) — sword that banished the Blaze of Disaster; wielded by the Sword Magess.
- **ARMS** — Agile Remote Mission Squad; trans-national anti-threat team. **ARMs** — dragon-fossil personal weapons.
- **Blaze of Disaster** — BOTH the legendary apocalypse AND the demon **Lord Blazer (ロードブレイザー)** who caused it. *(matches our decode: JP distinguishes ロードブレイザー = the demon vs. the kanji epithet = the event; EN merges them.)*
- **Cocytus** (コキュートス) — Odessa's elite officer squad (Dante naming).
- **Diablo Pillars** — 4 ancient monoliths powering the Heimdal Gazzo; functional until all 4 destroyed.
- **De Le Metalica** — alloy of the Valeria Chateau's hull.
- **Dianbai Guang** — express freight train under the Urartu Mountains (Guild Galad↔Slayheim).
- **Sword Magess** — wielder of Argetlahm (Anastasia; JP 剣の巫女). **Pillar** — the sacrificial medium role (ties to 生け贄/hero-sacrifice theme).
- **Slayheim Liberation Army** — resistance Brad/Billy served under Vinsfeld. **Treaty of Iscariot** — (in-world treaty).
- Others: Baskars, Empathite, Gias, Grauswein (monster dragon), Guild Galad, Guardians, Heimdal Gazzo,
  KnightBlazer, Kuiper Belt, Lombardia (airship), Mad Mountains, Mana, Mercs, Mt. Keltepekio
  Siltolingelon, Odessa, Raline, Raypoints, Sylvaland, Varukisas, Zoa Priest.
